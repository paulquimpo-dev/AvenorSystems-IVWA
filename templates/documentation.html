Your first workspace

  1. Choose a process, such as reviewing incoming orders.
  2. Define three stages: New, In progress and Completed.
  3. Assign one person to own each stage.
  4. Use the sample orders file to discuss the information your team needs.
  5. Agree on a weekly review of open work.

These are illustrative product workflows. The public website does not create or modify workspaces.

FLOW

Organize work

Give every item an owner, a stage and a next step. For an order review, move a record from New to In progress once someone starts reviewing it, then mark it Completed after the review.

SIGNAL

Understand progress

Count completed orders and review outstanding work. The sample CSV contains product, quantity and status columns so you can inspect a small reporting example.

Download sample CSV →

CONNECT

Keep context together

Use a consistent handoff: what happened, who owns the next step and when it should be reviewed. Keep sensitive information out of shared demonstration records.
